\begin{tabular}{|m{2.4cm}|m{5.2cm}|m{6.2cm}|}
    \hline
    \thead{Abbreviation} & \thead{Full Term} & \thead{Description} \\ 
    \hline

    CE & Cross-Entropy 
    & A standard classification loss used for supervised learning. \\ 
    \hline

    CLS & Class Token 
    & A learnable token used to aggregate representation for classification in transformer-based models. \\ 
    \hline

    CUB & Caltech-UCSD Birds 
    & A fine-grained bird image dataset used in this thesis, specifically CUB-200-2011. \\ 
    \hline

    DDP & Distributed Data Parallel 
    & A PyTorch training mechanism for distributed multi-GPU training. \\ 
    \hline

    FGVC & Fine-Grained Visual Classification 
    & The task of distinguishing visually similar subordinate categories, such as bird species. \\ 
    \hline

    HSDAS & Hard-Sibling Discriminative Attribute Selection 
    & The proposed attribute selection strategy that prioritizes attributes useful for distinguishing visually similar sibling species. \\ 
    \hline

    KL & Kullback-Leibler Divergence 
    & A divergence measure often used to compare probability distributions. \\ 
    \hline

    MLP & Multi-Layer Perceptron 
    & A feed-forward neural network module used for feature transformation or classification. \\ 
    \hline

    ViT & Vision Transformer 
    & A transformer-based neural network architecture for image recognition. \\ 
    \hline
